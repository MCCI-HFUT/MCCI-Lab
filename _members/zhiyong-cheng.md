**Prof. Zhiyong Cheng** is a professor in the School of Computer Science and Information Engineering at Hefei University of Technology.

His research interests include information retrieval and recommendation, social media analysis, and multimedia systems. He received his Ph.D. from Singapore Management University in 2016.
