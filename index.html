About MCCI Lab

Multimodal Computing and Cognitive Intelligence Lab (MCCI Lab) is based at Hefei University of Technology.

Building on multimodal computing, we focus on personalized recommendation, language and emotion understanding, and trustworthy intelligence. We investigate the theories and methods of personalized agents, social cognition, and the safety and reliability of intelligent systems.

Our work connects information understanding and personalized decision-making with agent interaction and collective social behavior.

Research Focus

01 / MULTIMODAL

Multimodal Computing

Representation learning, multimodal retrieval and understanding across text, vision, and behavioral information.
02 / PERSONALIZATION

Information Retrieval & Recommendation

User modeling, personalized information access, multimodal recommendation, and responsible recommendation.
03 / LANGUAGE

Language & Emotion

Language understanding, sentiment analysis, and cognition-aware human-centered interaction.
04 / AGENTS

Personalized Agents & Social Cognition

Agent-based social simulation, individual decisions, multi-agent interaction, and collective dynamics.
05 / TRUST

Trustworthy Intelligence

Fairness, robustness, transparency, and safety of personalized AI systems.
